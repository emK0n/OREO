OREO or ORE-NO!

Ten rounds. Five flavors each. Exactly three are real cookies that were actually sold or tested, and two are impostors we made up. Drag each cookie into Food or Fake, then check. Get all five right to unlock the story behind the real ones. Miss any and you'll only learn how many you got — not which.

Your result is judged on total checks and total time. Fakes get sneakier every round.

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere — your score lives only on this screen. Flavor list current to late summer 2026.
